Remotely Piloted Aircraft Systems News Archive

12/15/2006 The Microwave Systems unit of Northrop in California has awarded DRS Technologies a contract to produce lightweight EO/IR sensor systems for Class I unmanned aircraft in the U.S. Army's Future Combat Systems program.

12/14/2006 Robonic, a manufacturer of unmanned aircraft launching systems, intends to establish a European pilot training center for unmanned aircraft pilots above the Arctic Circle in Finland.

12/13/2006 The U.S. Air Force Research Laboratory (AFRL) is developing an active multispectral chemical detection sensor for use on unmanned aircraft that will patrol U.S. ports.

12/12/2006 Japan will develop a very small unmanned aircraft for surveillance and reconnaissance missions of enemy positions within Japanese territories.

12/09/2006 Britain announced it will launch a $245 million technology demonstrator program that will pave the way to deployment of unmanned combat aircraft by as early as 2020.

12/07/2006 QinetiQ of Great Britain converted a BAC-1-11 into a surrogate unmanned aircraft as part of an unmanned flight test of UAV swarm technology.

12/05/2006 The Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) wants assurances that composite construction methods will yield airworthy aircraft as unmanned aircraft systems gain credibility.

12/02/2006 Italy will purchase twelve RQ-11A Raven's for its deployed army forces for surveillance.

11/29/2006 Small unmanned aircraft are under investigation by the French Ministry of Defense for urban settings.
