The GT AERONAUTICS AT-6 Texan

The GT Aeronautics giant scale AT-6 Texan is a true scale ARF aircraft. The kit features an all-composite, vacuum-bagged fuselage, joined and ready to paint, with servo tray installed and engine mounts ready for your trusty Zenoah G-62. Simply mount the pre-built, composite, hollow-core stabilizer and rudder to complete the fuselage.

The excellent features of our kit include Kevlar skin-hinged flying surfaces, rubber band mounted landing gear (for those less-than-perfect landings!), internal flight surface linkages (except rudder), front and rear instrument panels and glareshields, canopy, the special "Spiral Flow (TM)" cowling for added engine cooling, pushrods and linkages, a pre-mounted, steerable tailwheel and instruction guide.

The wing kit includes a composite shell center section with rib structure pre-installed in the bottom shell. The outer wing panels are finished and ready to paint with the carbon-capped, helical-wrapped spar pre-installed. Fiberglass wingtips are included. By simply inserting the outer panels (spars) into the center section and gluing in place, the builder forms a full-span I-beam spar capable of withstanding the incredible G-loads of the giant scale air racing environment. Add the center section top shell and the wing is complete.

Whether you are racing, sport flying or scale flying, this tame WWII trainer will provide you with an impressive flying experience!!
