System Overview

The Long Range Counter UAS Base Protection System represents the next generation of defensive capabilities against hostile unmanned aerial systems. Designed to protect critical infrastructure and forward operating bases, this system provides comprehensive detection, tracking, and neutralization capabilities.

Utilizing advanced sensor fusion technology, the system can identify potential threats at extended ranges, allowing for adequate decision time and response. The system is designed to operate in various environmental conditions and can be integrated into existing command and control networks.
