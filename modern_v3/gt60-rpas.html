GT-60 RPAS

Commercial Aerial Platform

Training & Operations

The GT-60 RPAS is a stable, high-performance remotely-piloted aircraft utilized by GT Aeronautics for various applications, including flight instruction.

Alongside the GT-380 and GT-1500, the GT-60 serves as a primary platform for the RPAS/UAS Flight Training curriculum, helping students gain proficiency in external and internal pilot operations.

Detailed specifications for the GT-60 are currently being updated. Please contact us for more information regarding this platform.
