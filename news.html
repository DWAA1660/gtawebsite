06/17/25

Congressional Representative Harriett Hageman Visits GTA

GT Aeronautics had the distinct pleasure of hosting Congressional Representative Harriett Hageman and her staff at the GTA facility today for an in-depth brief on GTA UAS products as well as the current state of our country's Counter UAS capabilities.

Tom Rullman and Tiffine Canepa provided the brief, focusing on the highly successful development of the Bandito PGLM Counter UAS capabilities. The brief also focused on the current and future battlefield where small, low-cost UAS are being used to destroy multi-million dollar assets.

06/25/22

Heart Mountain Challenge

This weekend GT Aeronautics' President/CEO competed in the Heart Mountain Challenge, a long-range shooting match and fundraiser for the Special Operations Warrior Foundation. GTA hosted two Scout Sniper Marines and members of the Park County Search & Rescue.

The event was attended by 115 shooters. Entry registration fees support the Special Operations Warrior Foundation, which provides complete education funding to the children of fallen warriors.

06/22/22

Live-Fire Test of 35mm Rocket

Today, GT Aeronautics successfully completed the first live-fire test of a 35mm folding-fin rocket from a 4-rocket rocket pod. The test was performed to evaluate the ability of the 35mm rocket to successfully exit the rocket pod and the ability of the rocket "folding-fin" mechanism to properly deploy.

The test was an absolute success as the 35mm rocket left the rocket pod like a homesick angel, deploying its folding-fins correctly, and achieving its predicted target altitude.
