Employment Opportunities

Join the GT Aeronautics Team

GT Aeronautics is currently accepting applications for future employment in the following job descriptions:

RPAS/UAS Flight Crew: Pilot In Command

Description:

Responsible for the safe and effective operation of the aircraft system during all phases of flight.

Responsibilities:

  • Execute flight missions according to operational plans.
  • Conduct pre-flight and post-flight inspections.
  • Maintain flight logs and system records.
  • Coordinate with air traffic control and ground crew.

Requirements:

  • FAA Pilot Certificate (Private or Commercial).
  • Experience with UAV/RPAS operations.
  • Strong communication and leadership skills.

RPAS/UAS Flight Crew: Payload Operator

Description:

Operates the mission payload sensors and cameras to gather required data.

Responsibilities:

  • Operate EO/IR cameras and other sensor packages.
  • Monitor live video feeds and record data.
  • Coordinate target acquisition with the pilot.
  • Perform basic payload maintenance and calibration.

Requirements:

  • Experience with camera systems and sensors.
  • Attention to detail and situational awareness.
  • Ability to work effectively in a team environment.

RPAS/UAS Flight Crew: Visual Observer

Description:

Assists the pilot by maintaining visual line of sight with the aircraft to ensure safety.

Responsibilities:

  • Scan airspace for other aircraft and obstacles.
  • Maintain constant communication with the pilot.
  • Assist with launch and recovery operations.
  • Monitor weather conditions.

Requirements:

  • Good visual acuity and scanning techniques.
  • Understanding of aviation phraseology.
  • Safety-oriented mindset.

RPAS/UAS Flight Crew: Maintenance Technician

Description:

Ensures the aircraft and ground support equipment are in airworthy condition.

Responsibilities:

  • Perform scheduled and unscheduled maintenance.
  • Troubleshoot electrical and mechanical systems.
  • Assemble and repair composite airframes.
  • Document all maintenance actions.

Requirements:

  • Experience in aircraft or RC maintenance.
  • Knowledge of composites and electronics.
  • Technical troubleshooting skills.

Composite Technician

Description:

Fabricates and assembles composite parts for aerospace applications.

Responsibilities:

  • Layup carbon fiber and fiberglass materials.
  • Operate ovens and autoclaves for curing.
  • Trim, drill, and finish composite parts.
  • Prepare molds and tooling.

Requirements:

  • Experience with composite manufacturing processes.
  • Ability to read engineering drawings.
  • Manual dexterity and attention to detail.

Avionics / Systems Integration

Description:

Integrates electronic systems into aircraft platforms.

Responsibilities:

  • Install and wire avionics components.
  • Configure flight controllers and telemetry radios.
  • Test and debug system integration issues.
  • Design wiring harnesses.

Requirements:

  • Degree in Electronics or related field.
  • Experience with soldering and circuit testing.
  • Familiarity with UAV communication protocols.

Administrative / Contracts

Description:

Manages office operations and government contract administration.

Responsibilities:

  • Handle correspondence and scheduling.
  • Prepare and review contract documents.
  • Maintain compliance with regulations.
  • Support project management teams.

Requirements:

  • Experience in office administration.
  • Knowledge of government contracting (FAR/DFARS).
  • Strong organizational skills.

Ground & Flight Crew Training

Description:

Instructs students on UAS operations and maintenance.

Responsibilities:

  • Develop training curriculum and materials.
  • Conduct classroom ground school instruction.
  • Provide hands-on flight training.
  • Evaluate student performance.

Requirements:

  • Certified Instructor credentials.
  • Extensive flight experience.
  • Excellent teaching and communication abilities.
